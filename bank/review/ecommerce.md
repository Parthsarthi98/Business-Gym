## Facts verified on the web (October 2026)
- US duties (author doubt 1). The US has applied a 10% Section 301 duty to most Indian goods since 24 July 2026. It replaced the temporary Section 122 surcharge, which followed the 2026 Supreme Court ruling against the IEEPA tariffs. The duty is added on top of the normal (MFN) duty. The US$800 de minimis exemption has been suspended for all countries since 29 August 2025, and CBP made the suspension indefinite by interim final rules on 24 June 2026. A 2025 statute ends de minimis on 1 July 2027. I found no trade deal changing the 10% as of October 2026. c06 keeps "about 25% total" as an explicitly stated case assumption: a base apparel duty that varies by fabric plus the 10% Section 301 duty.
- FDI marketplace rules (author doubt 2). Press Note 2 of 2018 still governs marketplaces: an entity in which the marketplace or its group holds equity, or whose inventory it controls, may not sell on that marketplace. Press Note 3 of 2026 (issued 23 July 2026, operational from DGFT's notification of 5 August 2026) lets foreign-funded entities hold inventory only to export goods made in India. B2C inventory sales in India are still barred. The value chain had said "since September 2026", which was wrong and is now corrected.
- CCI (author doubt 2). The deal value threshold is ₹2,000 crore, with substantial business operations in India (for example, Indian turnover above ₹500 crore and at least 10% of global turnover). It has been in force since 10 September 2024 and was unchanged as of October 2026. The small-target exemption (assets ₹450 crore or turnover ₹1,250 crore) does not apply to deals caught by the deal value threshold. c11's brand (₹600 crore turnover, all in India) would otherwise have fallen under that exemption, so the c2 label now makes this point.
- UPI MDR. NPCI notified 0.4% MDR on P2M UPI payments above ₹2,000 from 15 October 2026. Payments up to ₹2,000 and small QR merchants stay exempt. "UPI carries zero MDR" was out of date and is now corrected. c03's 2% prepaid gateway fee is kept as a blended case figure because the ₹1,500 AOV is below ₹2,000.
