## Facts checked against sources
- Indian retail market. Redseer ("India's Got Retail 3", Mar 2025) puts it at about US$1.0 tn in 2024, with organised retail including online at about 21% (up from about 15% in 2018). KPMG, citing IBEF, gives ₹82 lakh crore for 2024. The old text ("organised modern retail roughly 10-15% of ~₹80 lakh crore") understated the organised share once e-commerce is included. It now reads "about a fifth of an approx. ₹80-85 lakh crore (about US$1 trillion) market (2024 estimates)". The FDI facts are correct and unchanged: single-brand retail is 100% automatic, with 30% local sourcing required above 51% foreign ownership, and multi-brand retail is capped at 51% with conditions.
- c04 UK VAT (doubt 4). HMRC VAT Notice 701/14 s3.3 lists "chilled or frozen ready meals ... for further preparation, such as reheating at home" as zero-rated. Hot food is standard-rated. The case was correct; I made the wording more precise.
- c10 jewellery (doubt 3). FY25 EBITDA margins are 6.6% at Kalyan Jewellers (consolidated) and 5.8% at Senco (SBI Securities), so 7% is realistic. Inventory days for listed peers are about 141 (Kalyan FY25) to 166 (Senco FY25), so 200 days was high. I changed it to 170 days and added a 15% gross margin, which makes the figure computable: about ₹1,500 crore (3,800 x 0.85 x 170 / 365 = ₹1,504 crore).
- c02 Hyderabad store (doubt 3). 2,500 x 60% x ₹900 x 250 = ₹33.75 crore, and 5,000 x 55% x ₹1,100 x 115 = ₹34.79 crore, for a total of ₹68.5 crore ("about ₹69 crore" is correct). That is ₹1,904 per sq ft per month, 5.3% rent-to-sales and 9.5% of catchment spend. DMart's FY26 revenue per bill is about ₹1,683 (HDFC Securities), so bills of ₹900 to ₹1,100 at a regional chain are plausible. Main-street rents in Kukatpally and Kondapur are ₹170-200 per sq ft per month for small units (Q1 2024 report), so ₹100 for a 30,000 sq ft anchor box is plausible. Unchanged.
