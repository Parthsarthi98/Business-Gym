## Facts verified on the web (October 2026)
- FDI in scheduled air transport: 100% is allowed, automatic up to 49% and by the government route above that. Foreign airlines may hold up to 49% (FDI and FPI combined), but only with government approval. Substantial ownership and effective control must stay with Indian nationals, and the chairman and at least two-thirds of directors must be Indian citizens. Security clearance is also required.
- Cape Town Convention: the MCA notification of October 2023 exempted aircraft, engines and airframes from the IBC section 14 moratorium. The Protection of Interests in Aircraft Objects Act 2025 was passed by the Rajya Sabha on 1 April 2025 and the Lok Sabha on 3 April 2025, and came into force on 1 May 2025. The Rules were notified on 30 January 2026. In insolvency there is now an Alternative A waiting period of two calendar months, after which Convention remedies (IDERA-based deregistration and export through DGCA) cannot be blocked. A creditor first declares the default to DGCA, which acknowledges it the same day and publishes it the next working day.
- Gulf bilaterals: Dubai-India has been fixed at about 65,000 to 66,000 weekly seats each way for over a decade. Dubai carriers use all of theirs, and India has signalled no early increase. I found no reliable source on how much of the Indian-side entitlement is used, so the claim that Indian entitlements are "partly unused" was removed.
- ATF is still outside GST (June 2026). In 2026 a West Asia conflict pushed ATF above ₹1,00,000 per kl, and Maharashtra and Delhi cut ATF VAT to 7% for six months from May 2026. Fuel is about 35 to 40% of Indian airline costs. IndiGo's FY25 fuel share was 34%.
- IndiGo FY25: 118.6m passengers, load factor 86%, RASK ₹5.14, CASK ₹4.66 (a unit margin of ₹0.48, inside the stated ₹0.2 to 0.6). Q4 FY25 ancillary revenue was ₹2,152 crore, or about ₹690 per passenger.
- Domestic traffic in 2025 was 166.9m passengers, so "about 165 million" in c02 stands. IndiGo and the Air India group together hold over 90%.
- GST on MRO services has been 5% since 2020, and IGST on aircraft parts has been a uniform 5% since 2024 (supports f07 a3x).
- UDAN: VGF and route exclusivity for three years, with capped fares on about half the seats (supports c05).
- EU Ground Handling Directive 96/67/EC: at airports above 2m passengers, member states may limit third-party ramp and baggage handlers to no fewer than two, chosen by tender for up to seven years (c12 now says this precisely).
