## Facts verified on the web (October 2026)
- PCA for banks (revised framework, effective 1 January 2022, still in force): risk threshold 1 is triggered by CRAR below 11.5%, CET1 below 8%, net NPA of 6% or more, or Tier 1 leverage below 3.5% (4% for D-SIBs). 6.375% and 9% are the threshold 2 lines for CET1 and CRAR, not the trigger. Exit needs no breach in four continuous quarterly results, one of them audited, plus RBI's supervisory comfort. SFBs and payments banks are outside the framework. The 10.25% total-capital line in the draft came from the superseded 2017 framework.
- Priority sector: domestic commercial banks 40% of ANBC or CEOBE, whichever is higher (2025 PSL Directions, from 1 April 2025). SFBs were cut from 75% to 60% in June 2025. The value-chain wording now says "domestic commercial banks" and adds "other listed sectors", because the 40% is not only agriculture, small firms and weaker sections.
- Credit lines on UPI: scheduled commercial banks since September 2023, SFBs added later. NBFCs still cannot offer them. RBI's June 2026 directions confirm these lines follow normal prudential, KYC and NPA rules. c08 is correct and now says "since 2023".
- Gold loans: RBI's consolidated gold and silver lending directions (June 2025, in force April 2026) standardise valuation, LTV (higher LTV for small loans) and auctions. "Tightened" was only partly true, so it now reads "standardise".
- Bank loans to NBFCs: RBI raised the risk weights in November 2023 and reversed the rise in February 2025, effective April 2025 (used in c11).
