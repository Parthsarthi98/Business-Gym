## Facts verified on the web (October 2026)
- ART (Regulation) Act 2021: section 21(g) still limits ART to women aged 21-50 and men aged 21-55. The Supreme Court refused interim relief in February 2025. In October 2025 (Vijaya Kumari) it exempted couples with embryos frozen before 25 January 2022, but only from the Surrogacy Act's age rules. The Gujarat HC (September 2026) let a couple proceed where one spouse was within the limit. The limits are therefore in force, with narrow court-made exceptions. Section 27(4) (an egg donor may donate once in her life) stands; its validity is a question pending before the Supreme Court.
- E-pharmacy: the 2018 draft rules (GSR 817(E)) were never notified, and government answers in July 2025 confirmed there is still no final regulation. The Delhi and Madras HC litigation continues, and online sellers operate under the general Drugs and Cosmetics Rules (prescription and pharmacist requirements). c08's "runs under draft rules" was wrong because draft rules have no force. It now says there are no final rules.
- Compulsory licensing and patents: section 84 allows an application three years after grant on grounds that include not being available at a reasonably affordable price (Natco v Bayer, 2012, the only section 84 licence). Section 3(d) bars patents on new forms of known substances without enhanced efficacy (Novartis, 2013). It is a ground for opposition or revocation and is not triggered by price, which the draft wrongly implied. DPCO para 32 (2019 amendment) exempts new patented drugs from price control for five years from marketing in India, so "NLEM inclusion" as a near-term risk was wrong. The DPCO Amendment Order 2026 (30 June 2026) changes launch reporting and overcharging liability, and I found no change to para 32.
- IRDAI health master circular (May 2024): insurers decide cashless requests within one hour and give final discharge authorisation within three hours.
- GST on most medicines is 5% since 22 September 2025, and some lifesaving drugs are nil. The unit economics line "incl. 5% GST" is correct.
- National Health Accounts 2022-23 (released 2026): out-of-pocket spending is 43.4% of total health expenditure. It was "about 40%" and is now "about 43%" / "over 40%".
- ICRA FY2026 sample of 11 listed hospital chains: occupancy 63.5%, operating margin 24.1%. These were used to calibrate the s5 benchmarks.
- Day care (doubt 4): under IRDAI's standard definition, day care is treatment under general or local anaesthesia in under 24 hours. It needs a formal admission and is covered as hospitalisation, not OPD.
