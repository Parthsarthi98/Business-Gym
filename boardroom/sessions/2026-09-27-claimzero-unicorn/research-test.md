# Research test (after the run, skill v3 prompt)

One round-1 persona (platform product head) re-run on idea-v3 with --research on, to check that web search works for board members.

Result: 2 searches run, sources cited.
Queries: "India returns management SaaS platform Shopify Unicommerce Clickpost pricing integrations 2026"; "Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules grievance officer response time India".

Findings used by the persona:
- Returns management is already a product category sold by Indian order-management and shipping platforms (unicommerce.com/blog/top-returns-management-software-in-india; clickpost.ai/returns-management-software).
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules 2026: grievance officers must acknowledge complaints within 48 hours, give the complainant a copy of the recorded complaint, and resolve within one month. Checked by the main session: the amendment takes effect on 1 January 2027 (SCC Times, 14 September 2026; AZB & Partners).

Score: 3/10 as a standalone company, 6/10 as an embedded module. Blocking objection on the standalone framing.

New point not raised in the main run: the 2027 grievance deadlines are a "why now" and a sharper sales wedge than the AI claim alone. Note that 48-hour acknowledgement and one-month resolution were largely already required under the 2020 Rules (judgement), so the new pressure is enforcement and the recorded-complaint copy rather than wholly new timelines.
