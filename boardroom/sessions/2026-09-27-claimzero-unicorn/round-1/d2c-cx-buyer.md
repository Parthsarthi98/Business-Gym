2. Biggest risk: wrongful-rejection liability sits with the brand, not ClaimZero, and an AI auto-rejecting a genuine damaged-item or COD dispute at scale will surface on Instagram/X within hours, not days. My 60-person team already manages this reputational tail manually with human judgement calls; an automated rejection engine that gets even 2-3% of edge cases wrong at volume creates a steady drip of viral complaints I have to defend, with no clear indemnity or human-override SLA offered in the pitch. This is a blocking category of risk unless liability and override design are addressed upfront (judgement).

3. Amendments:

- Add contractual liability language: ClaimZero indemnifies the brand for demonstrable wrongful auto-rejections above an agreed error-rate ceiling, published as a per-merchant metric, not just an internal dashboard stat.
