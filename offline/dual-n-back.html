Feedback

Each trial shows a square and plays a sound at the same moment. Press A when the square is in the same place as N trials ago, and L when the sound is the same as N back. Press both if both match. You can answer any time until the next trial starts.

A session is 20+N trials, with 6 position and 6 sound matches in the 20 scored ones. Score is hits ÷ (hits + misses + false alarms). 80% or more moves you up a level; under 50% three times in a row moves you down. Levels run from 1 to 100.
